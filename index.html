A management consultant still walks into a room with a company that has to choose. By 2035 the numbers are already on the wall. The choice is not.

Six stations in one room

Pick a station.

These are six things a management consultant does now. In 2035, two of them finish overnight, two come back as a draft, and two stay in the room with you.

AI Does It · Task 01

Pull the numbers together.

Today. The consultant exports the client’s files, repairs broken rows, and lines last quarter up against the year before.

In 2035. This finishes overnight. The system joins the tills, the rent, and the staff hours, checks them against the source files, and leaves a clean pack on the wall screen. No new judgment is required. The rules were agreed last week.

AI Does It · Task 02

Write the status note.

Today. The consultant turns the week’s work into a short note a busy client can read on a phone.

In 2035. The note writes itself from the pack: what moved, what did not, what is waiting on a person. You read it in the morning. You do not build the table by hand.

AI Helps · Task 03

Frame the options.

Today. The consultant builds two or three real paths, with cost, risk, and what the company would have to stop doing.

In 2035. The model lays the paths on the table. You throw out the one that only looks brave in a chart. You keep the one the company can actually run. Nothing enters the client’s note until you accept it.

AI Helps · Task 04

Test the recommendation.

Today. The consultant stresses the idea. What breaks if the price moves, if a rival copies it, if the team is smaller than the plan.

In 2035. The model runs the cases and offers a rewrite. You decide which case the client must hear, and which case is a distraction dressed up as rigor.

Must Stay Human · Task 05

Hold the workshop.

Today. The consultant sits in the room, asks the question that was not in the pre-read, and stays with the silence after it.

In 2035. The model can brief you. It cannot tell who is afraid to disagree with their boss. The workshop stays a conversation between people who will live with the choice.

Must Stay Human · Task 06

Stay after the decision.

Today. The hard part is the week after, when a habit has to change and someone calls, unsure.

In 2035. A progress summary can be prepared for you. The chair by the window is still yours. Mentorship does not transfer to a model.

One working day

Tuesday, 14 March 2035.

A retail client in Tashkent is deciding which stores are full and still lose money. The six tasks of the job are spread across the hours.

Morning

08:30 · 25 minutes

You

With the partner, one sentence goes on the board: which stores are busy and still lose money. That sentence is the work. Until it exists, the three packs on the screen are just three neat piles.

Task 05 begins here · agreeing what the people in the room actually need

The model, before you arrived

Joined 1,840 days of tills, rent, and staff hours. Checked them against the source files. Wrote the status note. Flagged two stores where the rent case fails. It did not choose the question.

Tasks 01 and 02 · the numbers and the status note

Afternoon

11:20 – 16:10

You, with the model beside you

Three options are on the table. Option B closes the most stores and looks efficient. It also leaves a neighborhood the brand promised not to abandon. You cut B. The efficiency was a trick, and you are the person who will say it out loud.

You rewrite the opening line yourself. The draft was smooth and empty. Then you ask for one thing only: rerun the rent case on the stores you kept. You read every line before it can enter the shared note.

A figure in the staff-hours file is wrong for two sites. The model offers a cause and a fix. You check the cause against the store manager’s log, then you accept the fix. Close to the meeting, a fast correction is more dangerous than the error.

Tasks 03 and 04 · options and the test of the advice

What the model does in the day

Lays out the paths, marks the one that hides a promise, reruns the narrow case you asked for, and keeps the failed checks open. It does not press “send to the client.” That action is yours.

Evening

18:05

You

The founder stays after the others leave. She asks whether she is failing. You do not hand her the printout. You ask which promise the company still means, and you wait. Eight minutes of that is worth more than the whole pack.

Then the note. The question was never the commas. It was whether the people who run the stores will understand what was decided. You are the one who signs it. If the advice is wrong, they call you.

Tasks 05 and 06 · the workshop, and staying after

The model by this hour

The checks are clean. The list of changes is written. The night pack is attached. At the door of the signature, it stops. It has no right to sign.

The note

Signed at the end of the room.

Option A goes to the client: price and promise stay visible together. The packs, the cases, and the list of changes were prepared by the model.
